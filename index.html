Why Google Reviews Are Your Business's Biggest Asset

  • Dominate Local Search: Google's algorithm ranks businesses with more fresh, positive reviews higher in Maps and the local 3-pack — meaning your competitors get seen first if you're not collecting reviews consistently.
  • Digital Word-of-Mouth: Over 90% of customers check Google reviews before stepping into a restaurant, clinic, salon, or retail store. Your rating is your first impression — before they even walk in.
  • Signals You're Active & Trustworthy: A steady flow of recent reviews tells both Google's algorithm and new customers that your business is open, thriving, and worth visiting.
  • Honest Business Intelligence: Reviews reveal what customers love and what needs fixing — it's free market research coming directly from the people who pay you.
  • Beat Bigger Competitors: A well-reviewed local shop consistently outranks national chains in neighbourhood searches — reviews are the great equaliser for small businesses.
  • Boost Conversion Before They Visit: A business with 4.6★ and 200+ reviews converts significantly more profile visitors into actual footfall than one with just a few reviews.
  • Build a Lasting Reputation: Every review is permanent content — a good review from 2 years ago still builds trust for a customer reading it today.

Pro Tips to Maximise Your Review Collection

  • Strike While the Iron Is Hot: Hand the card immediately after a great experience — not at checkout, not via WhatsApp the next day. That 60-second window after a positive interaction is your golden moment.
  • Remove Every Barrier: Don't ask customers to "search Google" or "find our Maps page." Just hand them the NFC card — one tap, review open, done.
  • Make It Feel Personal: Say something like "It genuinely helps us grow if you share a quick thought." A warm ask converts far better than a generic request.
  • Acknowledge Every Single Review: Reply publicly — thank good reviews warmly and address bad ones professionally. Future customers always read how you respond.
  • Train Your Entire Team: Every staff member who interacts with customers should know when and how to offer the tap card. Consistency multiplies results.
  • Place Cards Strategically: At the billing counter, reception desk, dining table — anywhere the customer's experience peaks is the right place for the card.
  • Stay 100% Compliant: Never offer discounts, gifts, or incentives in exchange for reviews. Google detects and penalises incentivised reviews — authentic taps always win.
